OUR BACKGROUND · ABOUT US

We are researchers who love building experimental systems

We study animal behaviour in the laboratory and in the wild. For our own research projects, we have designed and built unique, purpose-built experimental setups tailored to particular species, scientific questions and experimental designs. Creating these systems is one of the parts of research we most enjoy.

You can explore some of the experimental setups we have developed for our own research on our DIY setups page .

Our work brings together scientific experience, previous developments in our laboratory and open-source hardware and software. These approaches make it possible to customise equipment, improve it as new questions arise and, depending on the application, reduce costs compared with commercial systems.

OUR PURPOSE · WHAT WE DO FOR YOU

You focus on the science. We take care of the engineering

Designing and prototyping experimental equipment can take significant time and require skills beyond a researcher's main field: electronics, woodworking, machining and cutting, 3D modelling and printing, programming and mechanical assembly.

Even when a method is described in a scientific paper or shared on GitHub, finding compatible components, accessing workshop tools, assembling a first prototype and troubleshooting it can delay experiments.

From your research idea to a working setup

OpenLab Systems can assess your experimental needs, adapt an existing open design or develop a new purpose-built apparatus, source components, prototype, integrate hardware and software, and test the resulting system. We aim to deliver equipment that is documented, maintainable and ready to evolve with your project.

Spend less time building equipment. Spend more time doing science.

PLANNED SERVICE · GPU ANALYSIS

OpenLab Compute

Powerful animal-behaviour analysis. No expensive GPU workstation required

Run open-source animal-behaviour analysis tools in preconfigured, versioned Docker environments on GPU servers rented from third-party providers.

Many research teams lack a dedicated GPU workstation or spend substantial time resolving Python, CUDA and software dependencies. OpenLab Compute is intended to make advanced video analysis easier to access: researchers can use prepared, versioned environments on compatible rented GPU servers, process recordings when needed and export results without maintaining their own high-performance computer. Repeatable environments can also make collaboration and re-analysis easier.

Less setup timeStart from tested software environments rather than installing complex dependencies from scratch.

BehaviourCam

BehaviourCam system photograph coming soon

BehaviourCam is a modular, portable video recording system for studying animal behaviour in laboratory and field settings.

Main features and planned capabilities

  1. Multi-camera recording. Supports recording from multiple cameras, with four cameras in the configuration shown.
  2. Synchronised recording. Can be configured to record an experiment simultaneously from several cameras.
  3. Interchangeable cameras. Camera options for daytime and night-time recording, high frame rates or high resolution.
  4. Flexible hardware. Built around Raspberry Pi 3, 4 or 5 computers.
  5. Graphical interface. Control recording, adjust settings and view a live camera preview.
  6. Local and remote control. Operate using a connected screen or remotely through a local network.
  7. Recording organisation. Choose output folders, project and test names, and saved settings profiles.
  8. Offline operation. Control devices and transfer files without internet access.
  9. Flexible power options. Mains electricity, power banks, Power over Ethernet or optional solar power.
  10. Portable design. Compact carrying case for computers, screen, batteries, network switch and cables.

Features and specifications are subject to development and hardware configuration.

Insect Activity Monitor

Insect Activity Monitor photograph coming soon

A flexible video-based monitoring approach for activity studies of fruit flies and larger insects housed in laboratory or semi-field cages. Camera placement, lighting and analysis depend on the species and enclosure.

Proposed features and capabilities

  1. Fruit fly monitoring. Record movement and activity patterns in suitable small-insect arenas.
  2. Larger insects. Adapt camera coverage and detection settings for moths, butterflies and other insects in cages.
  3. Laboratory and semi-field use. Configure the system for controlled laboratory conditions or sheltered semi-field enclosures.
  4. Long-duration experiments. Plan scheduled recording for activity rhythms and repeated observations.
  5. Flexible analysis. Export recordings and activity data for validation and further behavioural analysis.

Concept under development. Features, compatibility and specifications will depend on the final configuration and validation.
