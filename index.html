THE GAP

Great open methods exist. Reproducing them can still be difficult

Research hardware may be distributed across papers, repositories, CAD files, component lists and independent scripts. Laboratories can spend substantial time sourcing parts, printing components, configuring cameras, integrating software, calibrating hardware and troubleshooting before the first experiment begins.

We do that integration work — while keeping the research system understandable and reproducible.

OPEN BY DEFAULT

Reproducibility is part of the instrument

Where third-party licences and customer agreements permit, we aim to publish enough information for researchers to understand, reproduce and modify our systems.

  • Bill of materials and sourcing information
  • Assembly and wiring documentation
  • CAD / 3D-print files where permitted
  • Software configuration and workflows
  • Calibration and validation procedures
  • Clear attribution to originating researchers and projects
OUR PRINCIPLE

We don't want reproducible science to depend on a black box.

Open when possible. Tested when delivered.

Open-source components and third-party designs are used according to their respective licences. Original authors, laboratories, publications and repositories are credited. “Open by default” does not mean every customer project or third-party component can always be redistributed.

BehaviourCam

BehaviourCam system photograph coming soon

BehaviourCam is a modular, portable video recording system for studying animal behaviour in laboratory and field settings.

Main features and planned capabilities

  1. Multi-camera recording. Supports recording from multiple cameras, with four cameras in the configuration shown.
  2. Synchronised recording. Can be configured to record an experiment simultaneously from several cameras.
  3. Interchangeable cameras. Camera options for daytime and night-time recording, high frame rates or high resolution.
  4. Flexible hardware. Built around Raspberry Pi 3, 4 or 5 computers.
  5. Graphical interface. Control recording, adjust settings and view a live camera preview.
  6. Local and remote control. Operate using a connected screen or remotely through a local network.
  7. Recording organisation. Choose output folders, project and test names, and saved settings profiles.
  8. Offline operation. Control devices and transfer files without internet access.
  9. Flexible power options. Mains electricity, power banks, Power over Ethernet or optional solar power.
  10. Portable design. Compact carrying case for computers, screen, batteries, network switch and cables.

Features and specifications are subject to development and hardware configuration.
