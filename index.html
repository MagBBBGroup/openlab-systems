WHO WE ARE

Scientists who love building things

We study animal behaviour in the laboratory and in the wild. For our research, we have designed and built unique experimental setups using our scientific experience, earlier laboratory developments, and open-source hardware and software.

We genuinely enjoy creating these instruments — and want to make that process easier for other researchers.

Building a new research instrument can involve electronics, programming, 3D printing, machining, woodworking, sourcing components and multiple rounds of prototyping. Even published open-source designs take time and expertise to reproduce.
