ABOUT US

Scientists who love creating experimental setups

Our work began with a simple need: instruments that match the questions we want to ask.

Our background

We are scientists who study animal behaviour in laboratory and field conditions. For our own projects, we have created purpose-built experimental setups, drawing on our knowledge, previous developments in our laboratory and open-science hardware and software.

We enjoy the creative process of building, modifying and improving experimental instruments. It allows us to adapt each setup to a specific research question rather than forcing the experiment to fit the available equipment.

You can explore some of the setups developed for our research on the MagBBB SmartLab page ↗.

Why open science matters

Open hardware and software can make instruments more flexible and, in some cases, considerably less expensive than commercial alternatives. But accessible designs do not automatically mean accessible builds.

Parts can be difficult to source; documentation may be incomplete; and assembly often requires specialist tools and skills in electronics, fabrication, coding, 3D printing or machining. Prototyping and troubleshooting can take substantial time, even when a similar system has been described in a paper or repository.

Our purpose

OpenLab Systems helps bridge that gap. We adapt, integrate, prototype and test equipment, or develop new setups around a particular experimental design. Our goal is to provide systems that researchers can understand, maintain, modify and upgrade.
