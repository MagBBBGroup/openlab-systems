WHY RASPBERRY PI?

Why we chose Raspberry Pi instead of a commercial recording system

BehaviourCam is designed around the needs of an experiment, rather than the limitations of a fixed, proprietary recording package. Raspberry Pi provides a flexible hardware and software platform that we can adapt to different species, experimental designs and recording environments.

Built around your experiment

Choose the number and position of cameras, recording settings, triggers and acquisition workflow to suit your research.

Freedom to choose cameras

Use compatible camera modules for daylight, infrared, high-resolution or high-frame-rate recording, depending on the experimental requirements.

Open and adaptable software

Modify recording interfaces, automate experiments, integrate sensors and organise recordings without relying entirely on proprietary software.

Potentially lower costs

Widely available components can reduce hardware costs, particularly for experiments requiring several cameras or multiple recording stations.

Portable and scalable

Build compact standalone recorders or networked multi-camera systems, with local control and operation without an internet connection.

Repairable and upgradeable

Replace individual cameras, storage devices, power supplies or computing boards without replacing the entire system.

Designed for research, with realistic trade-offs

Commercial recording systems can provide dedicated support, validated performance and specialised hardware synchronisation. Raspberry Pi-based systems offer greater flexibility and control, but performance depends on the selected hardware and software. Frame rates, timing accuracy and synchronisation must therefore be tested for each configuration.
