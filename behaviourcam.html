LABORATORY SYSTEMS

BehaviourCam

BehaviourCam is a modular, portable video recording system for studying animal behaviour in laboratory and field settings.

Enquire about this system ↗

SYSTEM OVERVIEW

Features and planned capabilities

  1. Multi-camera recording. Supports recording from multiple cameras, with four cameras in the configuration shown.
  2. Synchronised recording. Can be configured to record an experiment simultaneously from several cameras.
  3. Interchangeable cameras. Camera options for daytime and night-time recording, high frame rates or high resolution.
  4. Flexible hardware. Built around Raspberry Pi 3, 4 or 5 computers.
  5. Graphical interface. Control recording, adjust settings and view a live camera preview.
  6. Local and remote control. Operate using a connected screen or remotely through a local network.
  7. Recording organisation. Choose output folders, project and test names, and saved settings profiles.
  8. Offline operation. Control devices and transfer files without internet access.
  9. Flexible power options. Mains electricity, power banks, Power over Ethernet or optional solar power.
  10. Portable design. Compact carrying case for computers, screen, batteries, network switch and cables.

Features are configuration-dependent and may change during development.

WHY RASPBERRY PI?

Why we chose Raspberry Pi instead of a commercial recording system

BehaviourCam is designed around the needs of an experiment—not the limitations of a fixed, proprietary recording package. Raspberry Pi gives us a flexible starting point for combining cameras, recording software and other equipment into one adaptable system.

01

Built around your experiment

Choose the number and type of cameras, their placement, recording settings and control interface to suit your species, arena and research question.

02

Freedom to choose cameras

Use compatible camera modules for daylight, infrared-sensitive recording, higher frame rates or higher resolution, rather than being tied to one manufacturer's camera range.

03

Open and adaptable software

Recording workflows and interfaces can be modified, extended and documented. Additional triggers, metadata or integrations can be developed as a project evolves.

04

Potentially lower equipment costs

Widely available single-board computers and camera modules can reduce initial hardware costs, especially for multi-camera setups. Total cost still depends on development, assembly, storage and support.

05

Portable and scalable

Compact computers can be distributed around an arena or combined into a portable recording case, with local-network control and optional offline operation.

06

Easier to repair and upgrade

Modular parts make it possible to replace a camera, computer or storage device without necessarily replacing the entire system, subject to hardware and software compatibility.

THE PRACTICAL DIFFERENCE

Customisable research tool, not a closed recording box

Commercial systems may be the right choice when a laboratory needs a validated turnkey solution, specialist support or guaranteed acquisition performance. We chose Raspberry Pi for BehaviourCam because many behavioural experiments require unusual camera positions, different lighting conditions, custom control and room for future modifications.

What we validate for each configuration

Multi-camera timing, frame rate, recording reliability, image quality, storage performance and power requirements must be checked for the actual hardware. Software-coordinated recording is not automatically equivalent to hardware-level frame synchronisation.

The exact features and performance depend on the selected Raspberry Pi models, camera modules and recording architecture.
