SYSTEM OVERVIEW

Features and planned capabilities

  1. Modular light source. Configurable illumination appropriate to the monitoring protocol and local regulations.
  2. Image-based monitoring. Optional camera recording for documenting moth visits or specimens.
  3. Trap configurations. Adaptable designs for light trapping or non-lethal photographic monitoring.
  4. Environmental context. Potential temperature, humidity and ambient-light sensor integration.
  5. Organised records. Timestamped images and structured files for later review or identification.

Features are configuration-dependent and may change during development.
