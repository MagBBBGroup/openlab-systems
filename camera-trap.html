SYSTEM OVERVIEW

Features and planned capabilities

  1. Motion-triggered capture. Planned support for movement-triggered photographs and video.
  2. Night monitoring. Infrared-capable camera and illumination options for low-light deployments.
  3. Recording schedules. Configurable operating periods and capture settings.
  4. Outdoor deployment. Weather-resistant enclosures, suitable mounting and local storage.
  5. Power flexibility. Mains, battery and potential solar-assisted configurations depending on deployment.

Features are configuration-dependent and may change during development.
