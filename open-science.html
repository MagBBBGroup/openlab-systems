OPEN SCIENCE

Reproducibility is part of the instrument

We believe research tools should be understandable, adaptable and documented wherever licensing permits.

Open when possible. Tested when delivered.

Open-source methods should make experiments easier to reproduce, not create a new black box.

Where third-party licences and customer agreements permit, we aim to share component lists, assembly instructions, software configurations, CAD files, calibration procedures and clear attribution to the originating researchers.

Not every third-party design or custom customer project can be redistributed. We respect licensing, authorship and project-specific confidentiality.
