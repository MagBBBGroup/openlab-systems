SYSTEM OVERVIEW

Features and planned capabilities

  1. Integrated camera. Capture wildlife images or video with configurable recording modes.
  2. Microphone. Record animal vocalisations and environmental sounds alongside visual observations.
  3. Weather station. Incorporate temperature, humidity and other compatible meteorological sensors.
  4. Coordinated data. Store recordings and weather observations with consistent timestamps.
  5. Remote deployment. Plan weather-resistant housing, local storage and battery or solar-assisted power for several days of unattended operation, subject to power-budget testing.

Features are configuration-dependent and may change during development.
